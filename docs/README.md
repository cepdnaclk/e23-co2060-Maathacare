# MaathaCare
### Smart Digital Pregnancy Support System

**Connecting Mothers, Public Health Midwives, and Healthcare Administrators Through Digital Maternal Healthcare**

![MaathaCare Cover]({{ '/data/cover_page.jpg' | relative_url }})

MaathaCare is a digital maternal healthcare platform designed to improve pregnancy care management in Sri Lanka. It connects pregnant mothers, Public Health Midwives (PHMs), and healthcare administrators through a centralized system.

The platform supports pregnancy monitoring, appointment management, symptom recording, medical information management, and healthcare communication.

## The Problem

Sri Lanka has an established maternal healthcare system. However, many maternal healthcare activities still involve paper-based records and manual communication.

These processes can create difficulties such as:

- Managing pregnancy-related medical information.
- Keeping track of clinic appointments.
- Recording and reviewing pregnancy symptoms.
- Communicating between mothers and healthcare workers.
- Monitoring maternal healthcare activities efficiently.

A centralized digital platform can help organize these activities and improve access to relevant information.

---

## Our Solution

MaathaCare provides an integrated digital platform designed for three main user groups.

### Pregnant Mothers

A mobile application that helps expectant mothers manage pregnancy-related information and daily healthcare activities.

**Main Features:**
- Pregnancy progress tracking
- Clinic appointment management
- Daily symptom recording
- Digital health locker
- Pregnancy milestones and educational content
- Push notifications and reminders
- Multilingual support

### Public Health Midwives (PHMs)

A dedicated interface that supports maternal healthcare monitoring and coordination.

**Main Features:**
- View registered mothers
- Access relevant maternal information
- Review reported symptoms
- Manage appointments and clinic activities
- Support reminders and maternal follow-up

### Healthcare Administrators

A web-based administrative portal supporting system management and healthcare coordination.

**Main Features:**
- User and system management
- Administrative monitoring
- Centralized healthcare information management

## Key Features

### 1. Digital Health Locker

Provides digital storage and access to pregnancy-related medical reports and documents.

### 2. Smart Clinic Scheduling

Supports clinic appointment management and reminders, helping mothers organize upcoming healthcare visits.

### 3. Pregnancy Milestones

Provides pregnancy progress information, week-by-week milestones, and educational resources.

### 4. Symptom Tracking

Allows mothers to record pregnancy-related symptoms and maintain a symptom history for subsequent review.

### 5. Smart Push and SMS Notifications

Supports reminders and notifications related to clinic appointments, symptoms, and maternal healthcare activities.

### 6. Multilingual Support

Supports English, Sinhala, and Tamil to improve accessibility for users from different language backgrounds.

---

## How MaathaCare Works

MaathaCare connects mothers and healthcare workers through a centralized digital system.

**Step 1: Mother Registration**

Pregnant mothers create an account and maintain their pregnancy-related information.

**Step 2: Pregnancy Monitoring**

Mothers use the mobile application to track pregnancy progress, record symptoms, and manage healthcare information.

**Step 3: PHM Monitoring**

Public Health Midwives access relevant maternal information and support healthcare follow-up.

**Step 4: Healthcare Coordination**

The system supports clinic scheduling, notifications, and administrative coordination.

## System Architecture

MaathaCare follows a three-tier architecture consisting of the frontend applications, backend services, and database layer.
