# MaathaCare Platform Features

MaathaCare is a digital maternal healthcare platform designed to connect pregnant mothers, Public Health Midwives (PHMs), and healthcare administrators.

The platform brings together pregnancy monitoring, appointment scheduling, symptom tracking, medical information management, and healthcare communication.

## Mother Mobile Application

The MaathaCare mother application provides a
user-friendly interface for managing pregnancy-related
information and daily healthcare activities.

### Main Functions

- Pregnancy progress monitoring
- Clinic appointment viewing and scheduling
- Daily symptom recording
- Medical document management
- Pregnancy milestone information
- Healthcare notifications and reminders
- Multilingual access

## How MaathaCare Works

MaathaCare integrates the three user interfaces
through a centralized backend system.

### Step 1: Mother Registration

Pregnant mothers register and maintain their
pregnancy-related information using the mobile application.

### Step 2: Pregnancy Monitoring

Mothers record symptoms, manage appointments,
and access relevant pregnancy information.

### Step 3: PHM Monitoring

Public Health Midwives can review available
maternal information and support healthcare follow-up.

### Step 4: Healthcare Administration

The administrative portal supports centralized
management of users and healthcare-related activities.
