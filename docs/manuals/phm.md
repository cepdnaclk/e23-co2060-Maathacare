# Public Health Midwife user manual

**Draft guide — validate workflows against the final PHM portal.**

## 1. Access

Open the designated PHM interface and sign in using an authorized PHM account.

## 2. Maternal information

Navigate to the relevant mother/patient view to review information made available to the PHM role.

## 3. Appointments and clinics

Use the clinic or appointment view to review scheduled visits and update records only where your account permissions allow it.

## 4. Symptoms and follow-up

Review any symptom history presented in the portal and follow your institution's established clinical escalation process. The application does not independently replace clinical judgement.

## 5. Notifications

Use any available reminder workflows according to the app configuration. Confirm whether SMS delivery and push notifications are active in your deployed environment.

## 6. Screenshots to add

Include the PHM dashboard, mother record view, clinic schedule and symptom/history view. Document the actual button labels for each workflow.
