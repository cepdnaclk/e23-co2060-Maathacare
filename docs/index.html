DIGITAL MATERNAL HEALTHCARE

Better connected care for every mother.

MaathaCare is a digital maternal healthcare platform connecting pregnant mothers, Public Health Midwives and healthcare administrators in Sri Lanka.

Supporting pregnancy monitoring, clinic appointments, symptom tracking and healthcare coordination through one connected system.

ABOUT MAATHACARE

Maternal healthcare is a journey. It should be connected.

Pregnancy care requires coordination between mothers and healthcare professionals. MaathaCare brings essential digital tools together to support that process.

01

Accessible Information

Provide a central place for pregnancy information, appointments, symptom records and supported resources.

02

Care Coordination

Connect maternal healthcare activities across the mother application and healthcare worker interfaces.

03

Organized Follow-up

Support healthcare workers with relevant information and appointment-related workflows for maternal follow-up.

01 / MOTHER APPLICATION

Pregnancy support in one place.

The mother application provides access to pregnancy-related information, appointments, symptom recording and other supported healthcare features.

  • Clinic appointments

  • Supported reminders and notifications
